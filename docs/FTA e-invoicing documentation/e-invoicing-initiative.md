# FTA e-invoicing initiative

This page provides a consolidated overview of the United Arab Emirates (UAE) Peppol e-invoicing initiative. It outlines the key technical, security, and strategic documents available, summarizes the implementation timelines, and serves as a central reference for the project. The information is synthesized from various internal resources located on Google Drive and Notion.

## 1. Overview of UAE Peppol

The UAE is transitioning to a mandatory e-invoicing system for all Business-to-Business (B2B) and Business-to-Government (B2G) transactions, leveraging the Peppol (Pan-European Public Procurement Online) network. The objective is to enhance transactional efficiency, improve transparency, and ensure tax compliance. The system will operate on a "5-corner" model, which incorporates the supplier, the buyer, their respective service providers, and the Federal Tax Authority (FTA).

3. Security & Compliance

Security is a critical component of the Peppol Access Point. The following areas are covered in dedicated documents:
Multifactor Authentication (MFA): Requirements for implementing MFA to secure access.
Data Encryption: Mandates for encrypting data both at rest and in transit.
Security Monitoring: Protocols for regular and ongoing monitoring of the security environment.
A UAE Peppol compliance checklist is also available to track and verify that all regulatory requirements are met.
4. Project Strategy & Timelines

Internal strategy documents and tasks are being managed in Notion to guide the project. The rollout of the e-invoicing mandate is scheduled in phases.
Date	Requirement
July 1, 2026	A voluntary pilot program begins.
January 1, 2027	Mandatory for businesses with annual revenue of 50M AED or more.
July 1, 2027	Mandatory for all businesses.
 
Failure to comply by the deadlines will lead to penalties, including fines for each non-compliant invoice and for not using an accredited service provider.
